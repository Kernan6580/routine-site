Routine Life

Privacy policy

Routine keeps your information on your iPhone. We don't collect it, and we have no servers to send it to.

Effective November 1, 2026

The short version

Routine Life ("Routine", "the app") is made by Daniel Kernan ("we"). The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing you enter or that the app reads from your iPhone is sent to us. The only network requests the app makes go to Apple, to fetch weather and drive times.

What the app uses, and why

InformationUsed forWhere it stays
Calendar eventsTurning an event with an address into a leave-by time, and listing your day in the morning note.On your iPhone. Read through Apple's EventKit with your permission.
LocationNoticing when you leave home or arrive at a place you chose (such as a store) so a reminder can fire there. Working out drive time to an event.On your iPhone. Only the places you picked are monitored. Coordinates are sent to Apple Maps for drive-time estimates and to Apple Weather for forecasts, under Apple's privacy terms.
Microphone and speechTurning what you say while holding the mic button into a reminder.Transcribed on your iPhone. Audio is not stored and is not sent anywhere.
Reminders you addThe things you ask Routine to remember, and how you responded to its nudges (Done, Not useful, snoozed), which tunes how often it interrupts.On your iPhone, in the app's own storage and the shared storage its widget reads.
NotificationsDelivering reminders, the morning note and the evening check-in.Scheduled locally on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

What we don't do

We don't collect, store, sell or share personal information. We don't use third-party analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs. We don't track you across apps or websites. We don't create a profile of you.

Apple services

Weather comes from Apple Weather (WeatherKit) and drive times from Apple Maps. Those requests include the relevant coordinates and are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Speech recognition and the text parsing that turns a sentence into a reminder run on the iPhone using Apple's on-device frameworks.

Your data, your controls

From the app's Control screen you can export everything as a file, delete everything, or turn off any permission. Deleting the app removes all of its data. Permissions for calendar, location, microphone and speech can be changed at any time in Settings on your iPhone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them. Since the app collects nothing from anyone, there is nothing for us to delete, but if you have a concern, contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. A change that affects how the app handles your information will also be noted in the app's release notes.
